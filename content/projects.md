<div class="app-project-about">

**Memory of Trees**

Memory of Trees is a shared ASCII forest that all visitors contribute to, to protect the forest's memories. Blow a leaf to explore the forest, then watch it fall around a cut trunk whose rings spell out real wildfire statistics.

Humans are caretakers and the #1 risk to the forest's health. Each visitor becomes a source of heat or water: their real weather, time online, tabs and device strain feed a shared fire, and only rain and humid conditions can put it out. If visitors are not careful, it may all burn away.

<div class="app-project-about">

**SATpedia**

The sky is a commons, and so is Wikipedia. SATpedia links them: when a scientific satellite passes overhead, an article related to the purpose of its mission opens; when a commercial one passes, the text is disrupted by missing words and static, or a streak of radio emission is left across the images.

Megaconstellations don't rewrite what we know, they interfere with how we gather it. Visitors can plug in USB sticks to save the page before it gets destroyed. Behind it lies a simple question: how many satellites do we want in orbit, and for what purpose?

[Code on GitHub →](https://github.com/Bureau1217/head.mmd.workshop.touch_grass.group2)

<div class="app-project-about">

**cropTCHA**

Every day, millions of people solve CAPTCHAs without thinking about it: unpaid work that trains AI. The food we eat is often picked by underpaid, undocumented workers whose labor stays just as invisible.

cropTCHA senses light, blue and movement through your phone's camera and gyroscope, and turns them into sun, water and wind. By making you tend digital crops with real light, color and movement, it brings that hidden labor back into the field, where both harvests meet. Your reward: half a tomato, as a 3D file.

[Code on GitHub →](https://github.com/Bureau1217/head.mmd.workshop.touch_grass.group3)

<div class="app-project-about">

**Browsy**

Browsy is a Chrome extension: a small glowing creature who feels how you browse (mouse speed, typing, clicking, scrolling, tab-hopping) and gets overwhelmed when you rush. Switching tabs every few seconds feels normal to us; for Browsy it's deadly.

Slow down and he relaxes. Keep rushing and he closes your tabs, rips them in half, and more. A bad internet connection makes him even more sensitive. And if he dies, he takes your tabs with him.

<div class="app-project-about">

**PATATATTAQUE !**

Overproduction is a weapon, so they turned it against those who produce it. Surplus potatoes, wired in series, charge a supercapacitor; each time it is full, the energy is released and attacks the website of BL Tubercules Labs, replacing its content with protest messages. Visitors join the BLTL-GUEST Wi-Fi to watch it happen.

The more potatoes, the faster the attacks; as long as there is overproduction, the potatoes attack. "Against overproduction, let's organize the surplus!"
